Email OutsideGuide

Contact OutsideGuide

Email outsideguideapp@gmail.com for app help, listing corrections, privacy questions or content and rights concerns. Include the event or venue name, the relevant source link, and a brief description when useful.

You choose what to send. Do not include passwords, card details, private information about other people or unnecessary location details. The app does not automatically attach your saved guide, drafts or feedback.

A problem on your night out

For entry, payment, refunds or a booking, contact the venue, organizer or ticket provider using the listing’s original links. For immediate danger or urgent medical help, contact local emergency services; OutsideGuide support is not an emergency service.

Your private guide

Browsing does not require an account. To remove your local guide, open My Guide → Help & policies → Your data. To delete a signed-in account and its sign-in access, open My Guide → Manage account & deletion. Local guide data does not sync between devices.

Contact: outsideguideapp@gmail.com
