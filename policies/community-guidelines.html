Community guidelines

Useful information. Respect for the people outside.

Updated October 28, 2026

Share only what you know

Identify the exact event, venue or room and when you observed something. Distinguish what you personally experienced from an announcement or someone else’s report. Never invent a wait, cover, music style, security presence or attendance.

Confirm an existing detail when it is still accurate; send a new update when it changes or something useful is missing. Keep host announcements and independent attendee observations distinct.

Protect other people

Do not send someone’s private contact details, precise movements, identifying descriptions or sensitive personal information. Do not harass, discriminate, threaten, sexualize or rate people’s bodies. Do not infer identities or community labels from appearances.

OutsideGuide does not provide live video, recording, video uploads or video playback. Share a public source link and a brief factual description when it helps establish a listing. Do not submit footage or private stories to us.

Corrections, rights and review

Report inaccurate listings, harmful content or rights concerns through Support. Include the listing or source link and the specific issue, with only the information needed to investigate. We may correct or remove material after review.

This guest release keeps event drafts and feedback private on your device. Saving a draft does not send it to OutsideGuide. Use Support if you want the team to receive a correction.
