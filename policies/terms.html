Terms of use

A useful guide for planning your own experience.

Updated October 28, 2026

Using OutsideGuide

OutsideGuide provides venue and event discovery information. Use the app lawfully, respect other people and follow each venue’s rules. Browsing a listing or saving an event is not a reservation, ticket or confirmation that you attended.

You can browse as a guest or create an account. Protect your credentials and do not impersonate another person. You can sign out or initiate permanent account deletion in the app.

Plans, admission and safety

Schedules, performers, music, prices, entry rules and availability may change. Review the linked venue or organizer source before purchasing tickets or travelling. Age restrictions and identification requirements are set by the venue or event.

Listings and community descriptions do not guarantee admission, an experience, current crowd conditions or personal safety. OutsideGuide is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services if someone needs urgent help.

Tickets and external links

Ticket purchases, bookings and visits are arranged with the relevant venue, organizer or ticket provider. Their purchase, cancellation and refund terms apply. OutsideGuide does not sell tickets or collect payment for admission in this release.

Sources and responsible use

Venue names, photographs, flyers and linked social posts belong to their respective owners. Credits and links identify sources; they do not grant permission to copy or redistribute the material. Report an incorrect attribution or rights concern through Support.

Do not misuse the app to harass people, expose private information, misrepresent attendance or conditions, or interfere with the service. Community guidelines describe the standard for any information you send us.

Your guide and OutsidePoints

Saved items, preferences, drafts and feedback stay on your device. Keep any information you need elsewhere before erasing the guide or removing app data.

OutsidePoints are private demo points with no cash value or redemption offer. They do not guarantee gifts, payments, access, status or venue benefits.

Changes and contact

Features and listings may be corrected, changed or removed. These terms do not limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded. Updated terms will carry a revised date. Contact OutsideGuide at outsideguideapp@gmail.com for help or questions.
