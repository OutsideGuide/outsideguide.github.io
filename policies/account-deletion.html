Account deletion

You control your account and your local guide.

Updated October 28, 2026

Delete your OutsideGuide account

In the app, open My Guide → Manage account & deletion. Sign in to the account you want to remove, choose Delete account, and confirm. You may need to sign in again for this sensitive action. Linked Apple access is revoked before account deletion.

Deletion removes the OutsideGuide sign-in account and associated session and identity records, and erases this device’s local guide. You cannot undo it. If local cleanup fails after the account was removed, the app offers a cleanup retry and explains that the account has already been deleted.

What is handled separately

Device backups, another device’s local guide, emails already sent and records held by external ticketing, social or map services are managed separately. Contact outsideguideapp@gmail.com about correspondence or a privacy question; you do not need to send an email to initiate ordinary account deletion.

Guests have no OutsideGuide account to delete. Use Your data to erase the guide stored on a guest device.

Contact: outsideguideapp@gmail.com
