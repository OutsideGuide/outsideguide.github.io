Privacy policy

Your guide stays yours.

Updated October 28, 2026

Who we are

OutsideGuide helps you discover venues and events by music, mood and practical visit details. This policy covers the release dated October 28, 2026. Contact OutsideGuide at outsideguideapp@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Information saved on your device

Music, mood and city preferences, saved venues and events, attendance and experience feedback, event drafts and welcome progress are stored on your device. OutsidePoints in this release are calculated from that private feedback. These choices and records are not uploaded to OutsideGuide or published to other users.

Browsing as a guest does not create an OutsideGuide account or require your name, email address or phone number. Account creation is optional. Signing in does not currently sync or back up your guide; local entries remain on this device.

Optional account information

If you create an email account, your email address and password are sent securely to Supabase Auth to create and authenticate the account. Your password is not saved in your local guide. If you choose Apple or Google, that provider and Supabase process sign-in information, such as your account identifier, email address and any profile information the provider shares.

Sign-in sessions are kept in secure device storage. Apple revocation credentials may also be kept there so you can delete your account. These credentials are used for authentication and revocation, not advertising.

To delete an account, open My Guide → Manage account & deletion, review the confirmation and choose Delete account. The account service verifies your sign-in, revokes its sessions and removes the account. For linked Apple sign-in, Apple token revocation must finish before account deletion. Recent sign-in may be required to confirm this sensitive action.

Maps, images and external services

The guide may load maps, venue photographs and event artwork from their providers. Network requests can reveal information such as your IP address, the requested resource and technical connection details to those providers. Your device and platform may also collect diagnostics under your settings.

Opening directions, ticket links or social sources takes you to another app or website. Those services handle information under their own policies. A source link does not upload your private guide to that service.

This release does not request your precise device location, record background location, access your contacts, or record or upload video or audio. We do not use advertising tracking to build a profile from your local guide, and we do not sell your saved-guide data.

When you contact us

Support and reporting buttons prepare an email for you to review and send. If you send it, OutsideGuide receives your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond, investigate corrections or rights concerns, and operate the service. Do not include passwords, payment details, another person’s private information, or unnecessary location details.

Correspondence is retained while needed to handle the request and related service or legal obligations. Ask us about access, correction or deletion at outsideguideapp@gmail.com. Your email provider may retain its own copy.

Retention and your choices

Your local guide remains until you change or erase it, or your device removes the app’s stored data. In the app, open My Guide → Help & policies → Your data to erase saved items, feedback, demo points, preferences, drafts and welcome progress. Erasure is permanent within the app; other devices and copies in device backups are managed separately.

Removing local data does not delete emails you already sent or records held by ticketing, social or map services. Device backup, diagnostic and permission settings remain under your control. Private guide entries are not recoverable from an OutsideGuide cloud account in this release.

Account information remains while your account exists. Account deletion removes your OutsideGuide Auth account and associated session/identity records; it also attempts to erase this device’s guide. If a device write fails after account deletion, the app tells you and offers local cleanup again. It does not claim that your account still exists or that all local data was cleared.

Children and policy updates

OutsideGuide is not directed to children under 13. Venue age restrictions still apply to every visit, regardless of access to the guide. Contact us if you believe a child has sent us personal information.

We will update this policy when data practices change. Shared contributions or additional data collection require an updated policy and appropriate controls before they are enabled. The current policy is available in the app and in its public web copy.
